## 3. Semantic conclusions for Foundation review

The current product evidence supports the following bounded conclusions:

1. evidence-status separation, correction, and revisability are materially
   stronger than in the historical GOV-0009 snapshot;
2. ordinary interaction need not force an action;
3. product implementation details should remain product-owned;
4. hidden runtime authority through legacy consumers is a more precise current
   risk than old surface-level architecture labels;
5. Safety/privacy/rights enforcement still contains direct-audit unknowns;
6. no checked product result establishes Foundation conformance or general
   effectiveness.

## 4. Explicit non-claims

This Review addendum changes no Charter authority, RFC/Profile status, or
Kernel Draft status. It grants no CP2 authorization and does not claim:

- Family-Space general effectiveness;
- complete provenance/data-rights enforcement;
- public real-family evidence;
- that current Family-Space modules/fields are MingOS Core primitives;
- that Issue #17 is formally closed before owner review and merge decision.

## 5. Follow-up trigger

Re-audit again **on new evidence**, not on a calendar:

- when direct enforcement audits for provenance/data rights/escalation become
  available; or
- when real longitudinal family evidence materially changes what is known.

Until then, preserve the unknowns rather than manufacturing completion.
